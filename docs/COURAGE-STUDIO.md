# Courage Studio — design research and implementation

## Research direction
Reviewed current creative-web work from Codrops Creative Hub (https://tympanus.net/codrops/hub/), the TextClipScroll experiment (https://github.com/codrops/TextClipScroll), and the 2026 editorial collection discussing interactive comics and identity-led experiences (https://tympanus.net/codrops/category/articles/page/3/). The useful direction for Cocoon is editorial scale, illustrated worlds, purposeful interaction, and user-controlled motion. This is a focused survey, not a quantified claim about all web-design trends.

## Added experience
Courage Studio lives in Learn, with a new illustrated/editorial invitation on Home. Three authored moments each offer two exploratory choices: waiting for a ride, holding a boundary, and supporting a friend. Choices update the illustration, explanation, practical cross-link and source-linked downloadable note. There are no correct-answer scores, timers, rankings or requests for personal disclosures. Only the explicit Keep this thought button generates a local text download; selections are ephemeral and do not enter the demo store or transmit data.

Original SVG scenery uses the existing girl artwork, warm café lighting, architectural arches, seated company, message illustrations, foliage and lantern motion. Motion pauses offscreen, respects the OS reduced-motion preference, and has an explicit Rest the scene button. Controls stay outside decorative pointer layers. Editorial title uses a one-time clipped reveal. Existing four-left/four-right eight-scene navigation and its repaired close control are unchanged.

## Educational grounding
- RAINN safety planning: https://rainn.org/strategies-to-reduce-risk-increase-safety/safety-planning-for-survivors-of-assault-harassment-stalking/
- RAINN Consent 101: https://rainn.org/share-the-facts/consent-101-respect-boundaries-and-building-trust/
- WHO Listening when women need it most: https://www.who.int/news-room/commentaries/detail/health-workers-listening-when-women-need-it-most

The scenes are general reflection, not clinical instructions, guaranteed outcomes or a substitute for professional support. No message, call, location or incident is generated. Harm is never framed as the user's fault.

## Verification
All six branches exercised in browser; motion pause and reset checked. Reset disables export until another valid choice. Pocket-note tests check response/source alignment for every branch and reject invalid selections. Desktop and 390px mobile reviewed; supported-color contrast scan returned no issues in the current library view. No personal data was entered and no external communication sent.
