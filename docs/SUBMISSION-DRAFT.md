# Cocoon — submission access and testing draft

App: https://coco-safe-routes.lovable.app/

## Access instructions (paste into submission form)

Open the app and choose **Skip this and explore** (or **Let the light in**). No purchase, personal account or real emergency contact is required to explore the public demo. Use the atlas to move between Routes, Journey, Call, Kit, Reports, Learn and Circle.

For the main voice experience, open https://coco-safe-routes.lovable.app/presence. Choose a familiar role and voice, optionally describe a fictional ride/arrival, read the three acknowledgements, then choose **Try a demo call**. This is a real AI voice connection using the project's credits, with a two-minute guest limit. It requires microphone permission, internet, a supported browser and available demo quota. Guest quota is network-based, so people sharing a connection may share a limit. Do not use real identifying or sensitive information for judging. No payment or sign-in is required for the guest call. The collapsed **Scripted browser demo** is a separate simulated option if microphone access is unsuitable; it is not the live AI call.

## Suggested fictional walkthrough

1. Call: try a fictional cab-ride scenario with an AI playing a familiar person expecting you. The user understands it is roleplay; the AI cannot see, track, physically protect or contact anyone. End the call using the red End control.
2. Routes: choose **Use seeded demo paths instead** to explore three illustrative options independently of live routing availability. Adjust concern/distance/coverage weights and optionally include synthetic reports. This compares available paths, never guarantees safety.
3. Start a local journey demo. On Journey, expand **Local journey demo · simulated check-ins**, tap **I'm okay — check in**, then **I've arrived · end trip**. Fictional guardian updates and progress are clearly labelled simulated.
4. Learn: progress through a pocket guide or choose an illustrated Courage Studio option. Sources and country-specific legal context are linked.
5. Circle: search fictional conversations, inspect a thread, try a local reaction/bookmark. New contributions are local and held for demo moderation; this is not a live community.
6. Reports: move between sample observations. Optional live headlines are fetched separately and may fail or be paywalled. Kit offers local checklists, user-selected country contacts and optional external product searches; no purchase is needed.

## Limitations to disclose

Real private guardian invitations, SOS drafts, selected-recipient alerts and private journeys require consenting signed-in users and are separate from the no-account demo. Guardians must open their account inbox; no WhatsApp, SMS, background push, nearby broadcast or authority dispatch is connected. Stored/retrieved/read/acknowledged are distinct states. The two-way shared-location illustration is simulated. Do not judge it as real GPS tracking.

Live calls process microphone audio and chosen context through Lovable and its voice provider. Cocoon does not save audio/transcripts; provider retention policies apply. Captions are temporary. The app is not an emergency service, human responder or guaranteed safety system. Public map/news services and browser speech/media support vary.
