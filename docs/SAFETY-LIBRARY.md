# Safety Library

`/learn` is public to all demo roles, linked from navigation, home and Safety Kit. It covers consent/unwanted touch, younger people's body safety, warning signs, travel essentials, post-assault care and supportive responses. Practical options are never framed as a survivor's duty to prevent abuse. Education is paraphrased with source links to RAINN, NSPCC and NHS, reviewed 16 October 2026. Country-specific services and legal rules must not be presented as universal.

## Current news

`GET /api/public/safety-news?region=global|india` fetches fixed English Google News RSS searches server-side. No API key, user location or free-text query is used. Headlines are opt-in in the UI, source/date-labelled, linked through Google News and kept separate from route reports. Feed scope is the last 30 days and a limited publisher list, so it is neither comprehensive nor a ranking of seriousness. No article body, description or image is stored or rendered.

The server validates XML, rejects document/entity declarations, bounds body size to 500 KB, caps output to 12 items, filters unapproved publishers/URLs, removes markup, rejects invalid dates and deduplicates titles. Requests time out after 8 seconds and share in-flight work. Successful results cache for 5 minutes; provider failure can expose a clearly marked last-good list no older than 24 hours. Retrieval time is not rewritten on stale output. News checks every 10 minutes while enabled and the page is visible; a manual refresh respects server cache. Failed requests show an unavailable state, not fabricated stories.
